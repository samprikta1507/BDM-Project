# 🍽️ Restaurant Sales Analytics (BDM Project)

## 📌 Overview

This repository contains my **Business Data Management (BDM)** project focused on analyzing restaurant sales data to uncover meaningful business insights. The project follows a structured approach from problem identification to data analysis and final recommendations.

The goal is to support better decision-making by identifying **sales trends, peak demand periods, and high-performing items**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze daily and weekly sales patterns
* Identify peak sales days and time periods
* Determine high-demand menu items
* Evaluate revenue contribution of items
* Provide actionable business recommendations

## 🛠️ Methodology

1. **Data Collection & Understanding**

   * Examined datasets: item sales and raw materials

2. **Data Cleaning**

   * Removed inconsistencies and missing values
   * Standardized formats

3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**

   * Analyzed sales trends over time
   * Identified peak demand days
   * Compared item-wise performance

4. **Data Visualization**

   * Graphs and charts to highlight key insights

5. **Insights & Recommendations**

   * Business suggestions based on analysis

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* Certain days of the week show significantly higher sales
* A small set of items contributes to a large portion of revenue
* Demand patterns vary across different time periods
* Identified opportunities for inventory and menu optimization
